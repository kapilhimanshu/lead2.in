01 / Sales Organization

Sales Organization & Coverage Model Design

Do we have the right number of salespeople, in the right roles, covering the right customers and markets, with the right reporting structure?

Common symptoms
  • Territories with very different opportunity sizes
  • Unclear roles between hunters, farmers and key-account managers
  • Overlapping customer ownership
  • Senior salespeople servicing low-value opportunities
  • Inadequate management layers or excessive span of control
  • Incorrect sales capacity and inconsistent regional productivity
  • Poor industry or geographic segmentation
  • Compensation plans reinforcing the wrong behaviours
Deliverables
  • Current-state Sales Organization Diagnostic
  • Recommended Organization Chart
  • Territory and Account Allocation Model
  • Role Charters
  • Span-of-Control Recommendations
  • Sales Capacity Plan
  • Management Layer Design
  • 90/180/365-day Transition Plan
02 / Sales Compensation

Sales Compensation & Incentive Framework

Companies often create incentive schemes around what is easy to measure rather than what actually creates value.

Common symptoms
  • Salespeople earning incentives despite poor margins
  • Too much focus on acquisition and insufficient retention
  • Salespeople delaying deals to future quarters
  • Managers receiving disproportionately high payouts
  • Excessively complicated schemes employees do not understand
  • Targets disconnected from territory potential
  • No differentiation between new business and renewals
Engagement focus

Build a compensation system that aligns:

Corporate objectives→ Sales behaviour→ Individual performance→ Rewards
